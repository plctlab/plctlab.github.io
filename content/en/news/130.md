On November 7, 2025, the *RISC-V Campus Tour* event was successfully held at the Nanhu Campus of China University of Mining and Technology. Organized by the School of Computer Science and Technology of China University of Mining and Technology and co-organized by the PLCT Lab of the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences, the event focused on RISC-V open-source ecosystem development and technical practice. It brought together experts, scholars, and students to share in-depth insights on topics such as the RISC-V ecosystem, development tools, community participation, and learning pathways.
