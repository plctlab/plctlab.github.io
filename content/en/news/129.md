On November 2, 2025, the *RISC-V Campus Tour* event was successfully held at Ningxia Institute of Science and Technology. The program focused on popularizing RISC-V open-source technology and empowering beginners, bringing together experts in the RISC-V field along with university faculty and students. Discussions revolved around key topics such as entry-level resources, open-source practice, and in-depth technical analysis, building a bridge from theoretical understanding to practical participation and helping students quickly integrate into the RISC-V ecosystem.
